# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Focus reviews on observable behavior and evidence. Different levels of Windows experience are welcome.

Do not post private desktop screenshots, credentials or personal folder paths. Do not propose fixes that silently terminate Explorer or change unrelated desktop state.

Repeated harassment, disclosure of private information or deliberately disruptive contributions may be removed by repository maintainers.

請尊重其他參與者，以可重現行為與驗證結果討論問題。避免公開私人桌面資料；修正不得默默終止 Explorer 或更動無關的桌面狀態。
